Todd Personal Coach Privacy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Purpose

This private coaching service is used by Todd Blackhurst to store and retrieve personal health, training, nutrition, recovery, and workout feedback data.

Data Used

The service may process coaching messages, blood pressure readings, workout feedback, food summaries, body metrics, and related notes submitted by Todd.

Storage

Data is stored in Todd's private Supabase project and accessed through authenticated Netlify serverless functions.

Access

API access requires the private x-coach-secret header. Do not share the secret publicly.

Sharing

This service is not intended for public use or third-party sharing. It exists only to support Todd's personal coaching workflow.

Contact

For questions, contact Todd directly.
